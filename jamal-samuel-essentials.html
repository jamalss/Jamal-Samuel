What Counts As a Content Edit

A content edit is a small, straightforward change to existing information on your site. These count toward your monthly edit allowance:

Changing prices or service rates
Replacing or adding photos
Updating business hours or contact info
Adding or removing one product or service
Updating a promo banner or announcement text

Not a small edit: rebuilding a section, adding a new page, rewriting all copy, or changing the overall layout. These are quoted separately.

FAQ

Straight Answers. No Runaround.

Do I need any technical knowledge to get started?
None at all. Send us your logo, photos, business name, and what you sell. We handle the design, build, and launch. You share your link with customers and that's it.
How long does it take to get my website live?
Most sites go live within 48 hours of receiving your content. The faster you send your information, the faster you're online. We keep you updated via WhatsApp throughout.
How quickly do you respond to support messages?
Support messages are answered via WhatsApp during business hours, Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 6 PM, and Saturday 10 AM to 2 PM. Messages sent outside these hours are answered the next business day. Edit turnaround is within 48 hours for Launch and Link, 24 hours for Managed, and same business day for Scale.
Can I request something outside my plan, like a logo or extra page?
Yes. Anything outside your monthly plan — logo design, extra pages, booking systems, copywriting, flyers, eCommerce — can be added as a one-time service. All add-ons are quoted and agreed upon before work begins, so there are never any surprise charges. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll give you a price upfront.
Can I cancel my plan at any time?
Yes. No long-term contracts. Cancel anytime with no penalties. Your site stays live until the end of your current billing period. We earn your business every month by not locking you in.
What if I need to update my prices or menu?
Message us on WhatsApp with your changes. We update your site within 48 hours. Every plan includes monthly updates so that your conversion system always reflects your real business.
What if I don't have a logo or professional photos?
Most of our clients don't and that's fine. Phone photos work. We'll tell you exactly what to send and make it look professional. You don't need to be ready. You just need to start.
Do I need to buy a domain name?
All plans, including Launch, include custom domain connection. You purchase your domain (~$15/year) separately and we handle the full technical setup for you.
Can I upgrade my plan as my business grows?
Absolutely. Upgrade from Launch → Link → Managed → Scale anytime. Message us on WhatsApp. We apply the upgrade and update your site within 48 hours. No penalties, no paperwork.
What is the Loyalty Ladder?
The Loyalty Ladder is how Jamal Samuel Essentials rewards clients who grow with us. At Month 3 you get a free site review and a bonus update. At Month 6 you get a free promo graphic. At Month 12, the biggest milestone, Managed and Scale clients unlock full site ownership, and Launch and Link clients get one free feature from the next tier up added to their site. At Month 24 every client gets a complimentary full site refresh. Milestones are tracked from your start date. You don't need to ask, we reach out to you when you hit each one.
Do I ever actually own my website?
Yes! We also think that matters. Clients on the Managed and Scale plans who complete 12 consecutive months unlock full site ownership. That means the site files are yours to keep, even if you ever decide to cancel. From that point your monthly fee covers hosting, updates, and marketing only, not the build. On Launch and Link plans, your site files are available for transfer to you on request at any time. We're not in the business of holding your work hostage.
What happens to my site if I cancel?
Your site goes offline at the end of your billing period. We recommend downloading your content before cancelling. You can also ask us to transfer the site files to you directly.
