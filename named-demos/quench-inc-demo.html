Why Quench Exists

One founder's mission to change how an island drinks water.

Marla Nathania Foster spent 17 years in sales and business development before founding Quench Inc. in 2019 — built on a simple belief: that Saint Lucia's hotels, schools and homes shouldn't have to choose between convenience and the environment.

"We're not just selling bottles. We're building the infrastructure for a plastic-free Saint Lucia — one dispenser, one bottle, one household at a time."
