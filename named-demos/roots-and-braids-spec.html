About the Studio

Braiding Is Not
Just a Service.
It's an Inheritance.

Roots & Braids was born from a deep love of Caribbean hair culture and the art of protective styling passed down through generations. Our lead braider brings over 8 years of professional training and hands-on mastery in African and Caribbean braiding traditions — from knotless and cornrows to intricate Fulani patterns and goddess locs.

Every client who sits in our chair receives the same thing: precision technique, healthy hair practices, and a style that reflects who they are. We take the time to understand your hair type, your lifestyle, and your vision before we ever pick up a comb.
