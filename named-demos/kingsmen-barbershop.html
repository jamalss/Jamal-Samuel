Our Philosophy

Built Around Quality,
Not Quantity

At Kingsmen, we made a deliberate choice early on: we would rather serve fewer clients exceptionally well than rush through a full book. That decision shapes everything — from the way we greet you at the door to the care we take at the end of every cut.

Our barbers don't just follow instructions. They listen, they observe, and they apply professional judgment to give you a result that fits your face shape, your lifestyle, and the image you want to project. That combination of skill and genuine attention to the person in the chair is what keeps our clients coming back.

Consistency is our standard. Whether you've been coming to us for two months or two years, you'll leave looking just as sharp as the first time — if not sharper.

FAQ

Questions We
Hear Most Often

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in? +
We take both appointments and walk-ins. That said, we strongly recommend booking in advance — especially on weekends — because our barbers do fill up. Walk-ins are welcome on a first-come, first-served basis when chair time is available. Booking ahead guarantees your specific barber and your preferred time.
How far in advance should I book? +
For weekday appointments, 24–48 hours is usually enough. Fridays and Saturdays book out fast — sometimes days ahead. If you have an event, interview, or occasion you're getting groomed for, we suggest booking at least 3–5 days in advance to secure your preferred barber and time.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Zelle, and CashApp. Membership billing is processed securely through our online system. If you have a specific payment question, just ask when you book.
How often should I come in for a haircut? +
It depends on your style. For tight fades and clean lines, most clients find every 2–3 weeks keeps them looking sharp. For longer or more relaxed styles, every 4–6 weeks is standard. Your barber will recommend a maintenance schedule at your first visit based on your hair type and the cut you've chosen.
Do you cut children's hair? +
Yes, absolutely. We welcome children and take extra care to make the experience calm and comfortable. Jordan is particularly great with younger clients — patient, engaging, and precise. We recommend booking specifically for kids so we can plan appropriately. Kids 12 and under are welcome.
What if I'm not happy with my cut? +
Tell us before you leave. Our barbers want you walking out confident, and if something isn't right, we'll fix it. We don't charge for corrections done on the same visit. Honest feedback is welcome — we would rather know and fix it than have you leave unsatisfied.
