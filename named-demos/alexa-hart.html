Her Story

More Than The Game

07

Alexa Hart didn't arrive at the top of the league by accident. Growing up between Caribbean roots and an American court, she learned early that nobody was going to hand her a path — she'd have to build one, possession by possession.

The work ethic that now defines her game started in gyms before sunrise, long before scouts were watching. By the time they were, she'd already decided what kind of player — and person — she was going to be: relentless, accountable, and impossible to ignore.

Seven professional seasons later, the résumé speaks for itself. But ask her teammates what defines Alexa, and the answer isn't a stat line — it's the standard she holds the locker room to, and the work she puts in for the next generation long after the final whistle.

Today, Alexa is building something bigger than a basketball career: a platform for women's sports advocacy, community investment, and a business built on the same discipline that won her a championship.
