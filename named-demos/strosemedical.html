Gynecology
Cervical Cancer: Symptoms & Prevention

Cervical cancer remains one of the leading causes of cancer-related deaths among women in Saint Lucia — yet it is one of the most preventable when detected early. Know the signs:

  • Unusual vaginal bleeding
  • Pelvic pain or discomfort
  • Bleeding after sex
  • Abnormal discharge
  • Post-menopausal bleeding
  • Pain during intercourse
Learn more
Gastroenterology
Acid Reflux: More Than Just Heartburn

Acid reflux affects millions and presents in ways many don't immediately recognise. Don't ignore these common signs:

  • Chest pain
  • Burning throat
  • Bloating
  • Heart palpitations
  • Difficulty swallowing
  • Constipation
  • Sporadic coughing
  • Fullness feeling
Learn more
