Meet the baker

Meet Evy

✏️ TO BE EDITED: Drafted from what you've shared so far — add or change anything that doesn't sound like you.

Soul Crust started with a simple love of baking — sourdough especially — long before it was ever meant to be a business.

Now it's becoming something more intentional: a real home micro-bakery, made in small batches, right in Evy's own kitchen. No factory, no shortcuts — just a well-fed starter, real ingredients, and loaves shaped by hand the night before they're baked.

Every order is for someone whose name I'll know by pickup.

— Evy, Soul Crust by Evy

Why it tastes different

Slow Bread. Honest Ingredients. No Shortcuts.

Naturally Leavened

No commercial yeast. A wild starter and a long ferment do the work, which is why the crust crackles and the crumb stays open and tender.

Small-Batch Baking

A dozen loaves at a time, not a hundred. Each one gets shaped, scored, and watched — not run through a line.

Seasonal Ingredients

What's fresh locally shapes the weekly menu — so the focaccia in July tastes nothing like the one in November, on purpose.
