Our Story

Cooked the way it's always been done.

Every dish at Kyma starts the same way it would in a Mediterranean home kitchen — fresh herbs, good olive oil, and recipes that haven't changed in generations. We don't pre-make and hold trays of food. When you order, we start cooking.

  • Made-to-order, never sitting under a heat lamp
  • House-made tzatziki, hummus, and dressings
  • Vegetarian options on every part of the menu
