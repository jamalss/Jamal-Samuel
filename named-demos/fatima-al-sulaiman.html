How I Work

There's no one-size-fits-all in therapy

Everyone comes in with a different blueprint for life. Different stories, different walks of life, different things that have shaped them. Which means what heals you is different to what heals the person next to you.

My approach is designed specifically for your journey — not a generic protocol. I draw from multiple evidence-based modalities, but the real work happens in the relationship between us and the honest space we build together.

I'm also not for everyone — and that's completely ok. There is no single right type of therapy, and no single right therapist. What matters is finding the right fit for you.

A Different Way of Working

Internal Family Systems & Parts Work

IFS — Internal Family Systems — is one of the most powerful therapeutic frameworks available. Instead of treating your inner experience as a problem to fix, it treats your mind as a family of different "parts," each with its own feelings, beliefs, and reasons for existing.

The angry part. The inner critic. The one that shuts down. The people-pleaser. These aren't flaws — they're protective strategies that developed for good reasons. Parts Work helps you meet them with curiosity instead of shame.

Managers
Parts that try to control life to keep you safe — perfectionism, people-pleasing, over-achievement. They mean well. They're also exhausted.
Firefighters
Emergency responders to emotional pain. They go for immediate relief — sometimes in ways that create new problems. Not bad. Just reactive.
Exiles
Wounded younger parts carrying old pain, shame, and trauma — pushed out of awareness to protect the rest of you. They need care, not more exile.
Self — The Core of Who You Are
Underneath all the parts, there is a calm, curious, compassionate Self. It was never broken. IFS helps you lead from this place again.
