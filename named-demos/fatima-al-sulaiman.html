About Fatima

A psychologist who believes in whole-person healing

I'm a Licensed Clinical Psychologist specialising in trauma, emotional healing, and the relationship you have with yourself. My work is guided by the belief that every person carries within them the capacity for growth, resilience, and genuine wellbeing, even when that feels impossibly far away.

After completing my MSc in the Psychology of Mental Health at the University of Edinburgh, I trained extensively in trauma-informed care, Internal Family Systems (IFS), and somatic approaches to healing. I work with clients in both Arabic and English, offering a culturally sensitive space that honours the full complexity of each person's experience.

My approach is integrative — drawing from IFS, Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), and Dialectical Behaviour Therapy (DBT). But above everything, I believe that the therapeutic relationship itself is the most powerful vehicle for change.

Whether you're carrying the weight of past trauma, navigating anxiety, or simply trying to understand yourself more deeply, I'm here, and I'm glad you found your way here.

A Different Way of Working

Internal Family Systems & Parts Work

Internal Family Systems (IFS) is one of the most transformative therapeutic models available today. Rather than seeing your inner world as a problem to solve, IFS treats the mind as a family of "parts" — each with its own feelings, beliefs, and protective roles.

The angry part. The inner critic. The exhausted caretaker. The one who shuts down when things get hard. These aren't character flaws. They're protective strategies developed in response to pain and they deserve understanding, not shame.

Managers
Parts that work hard to keep you in control, organised, and safe from overwhelm often through perfectionism, people-pleasing, or over-achievement.
Firefighters
Parts that respond to emotional pain by seeking immediate relief sometimes through behaviours that create new problems of their own.
Exiles
Wounded younger parts that carry the burdens of pain, shame, and trauma hidden away to protect the rest of the system from being overwhelmed.
