What We Do

Three services. One clear mission: get your business in order.

Whether you are just starting out, drowning in data, or struggling with QuickBooks, Consult With Elle brings clarity and structure to your finances and operations.

Business Start-Up

Build the foundation right

Launching a business involves more than just an idea. Consult With Elle walks you through registration, structure, and setup so your business is legally sound and operationally ready from day one.

  • Business structure selection (Sole Proprietorship, LLC, Partnership)
  • Business plan development
  • Full registration paperwork — filed for you
  • Tax registration and basic business licence research
  • NIC registration
  • Business email and file organisation setup
  • Client intake forms and contract templates
  • Bank account checklist and QuickBooks initial setup
  • Website and Google Business Profile outsourcing
Consult With Elle does not provide legal or tax advice. Clients are encouraged to consult a qualified attorney or tax professional for guidance on legal and tax matters.

Data Entry

Accurate records. Clear decisions.

Accurate data management is not a nice-to-have, it is the difference between knowing your numbers and guessing them. We handle your records so you are always tax-ready and informed.

  • Receipt entry and invoice logging from email or physical copies
  • Correct categorisation of every transaction
  • Client and customer database entry — clean contact lists from spreadsheets and emails
  • Inventory and SKU data entry — new products, pricing, descriptions

QuickBooks Consulting

Set up right. Run with confidence.

QuickBooks is only as useful as the person who configured it. We set it up correctly, fix what is broken, and train you and your team to use it with confidence.

  • Full QuickBooks setup — chart of accounts, customers, vendors, invoicing, tax settings
  • Maintenance for existing accounts — fix duplicates, chart of accounts errors, unbalanced reconciliations
  • Transaction import and categorisation
  • Training for owners and employees — invoicing, inventory, bills, sales, reports, reconciliation
