Her Story

Built Across Two Continents

Gabby Williams' journey has never followed one lane. Raised between American and French influences, shaped by UConn's championship standard, sharpened across the WNBA and Europe, and elevated by Olympic pressure, Gabby has built a career defined by adaptability, defense, and identity.

Her 2026 move to Golden State marked a new chapter — not a step down in competition, but a step up in opportunity. Coaches now run the offense through her. The numbers, and the role, are both growing.
