The Ortega Family

Built In Memory.
Built For Community.

Three brothers. One vision. A love letter to the Miami they grew up in — and to the mother who taught them that community, culture, and a good meal could hold a neighborhood together.

Carlos, Alvaro, and Sebastian Ortega were born in Puerto Rico, raised by Cuban parents right here in the heart of Miami. They wanted their bodega to feel like a Puerto Rican-inspired New York deli — but with a Miami twist. A place with photos of Coral Gables, Key Biscayne, family on the walls, and the kind of hospitality where your order is already known when you walk in.

Good food at fair prices. No $20 bacon, egg and cheese. A local snack wall built from customer suggestions. Specialty items, always something new to try — and community that goes beyond the transaction.
