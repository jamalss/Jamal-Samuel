Our Story

Started on a corner. Built by a neighborhood.

Maple & Main opened eight years ago in a space half this size, with a single espresso machine and a folding table for two. There was no business plan beyond making coffee worth walking for.

What kept people coming back wasn't the menu — it was the corner itself. Regulars started leaving their coats on the same chairs. Staff learned orders before names. The café grew because the neighborhood asked it to.

We still roast in small batches, still bake before sunrise, and still believe a good café is judged by how it treats a Tuesday morning, not just a Saturday.

— The Maple & Main Family
