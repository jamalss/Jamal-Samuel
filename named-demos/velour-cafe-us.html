Our Story

Brewed with Purpose,
Served with Soul

Velour Café was born from a simple conviction: this neighborhood deserved a coffeehouse that felt like a destination, not a stop. Unhurried. Intentional. Deeply good.

We source single-origin beans from small ethical farms, roast in small batches, and train our team to treat every order — and every gathering — as a craft. Nothing here is rushed. Everything is made with intention.

Whether you're stopping in for a quiet morning espresso or booking the whole room for a celebration, you'll find the same warmth — because we believe the best things in life deserve to be savoured together.
