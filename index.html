Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build my website?

Most builds are live within 2–3 business days after Jamal Samuel receives your content: your menu or services, prices, photos, logo, and business details. More complex Premium and Enterprise builds can take a little longer.

What do I need to provide?

Your list of products, menu items or services with prices, a few good photos, your logo if you have one, your business hours, delivery or booking details, and the WhatsApp number orders should go to. If something is missing, Jamal Samuel will tell you exactly what is needed.

How much does it cost?

Every plan has a one-time setup fee plus a monthly partnership fee that covers hosting, updates, and support. The right plan depends on what your business needs, so message Jamal Samuel on WhatsApp for exact pricing. Prices are in USD, and 50% of the setup fee is required to begin work.

How do I pay?

Clients in Saint Lucia can pay in cash. Clients outside Saint Lucia pay by MoneyGram.

Is there a contract?

Every plan has a 3-month minimum, then continues month-to-month. After the first 3 months you can cancel at any time with 30 days' notice. The setup fee is non-refundable.

Who owns my website?

Once your setup fee is paid in full, your website files are yours. The monthly partnership fee covers hosting, updates, and support, not ownership of the build.

Do you work with businesses outside Saint Lucia?

Yes. Jamal Samuel works with businesses in Saint Lucia, Barbados, Trinidad and Tobago, Jamaica, Guyana, and other Caribbean islands. Everything is handled remotely over WhatsApp.

Can customers overseas order from my website?

Yes. Customers in the US, UK, Canada or anywhere else can browse your menu or services, see your prices, and send an order straight to your WhatsApp. A PayPal button is included on every plan so overseas customers can pay you online.
