What Jamal Samuel Sets Up for You

Every plan includes a complete Google Business Profile setup — not just creation. Setup without optimisation is a waste. We do both.

  • Business category selection — The single most important GBP field. Wrong category = wrong customers. We select the primary and secondary categories that match exactly what you do.
  • Local keyword business description — Written to contain the words your customers actually search. Not generic, not copied from your Instagram bio. Search-optimised from the start.
  • Services listed correctly — Google reads your listed services to decide when to show your business. We list every relevant service with descriptions so nothing is missed.
  • WhatsApp number & website linked — Customers can tap to WhatsApp you directly from your Google listing. We connect both so every traffic source points back to your ordering system.
  • Business hours set correctly — Including special holiday hours. Google penalises listings with outdated hours because it reflects badly on searchers. We get it right at launch.
  • Verification guidance — We walk you through the verification step so your profile is confirmed and active. Unverified GBPs rank lower and can be removed by Google.
  • Review request strategy — We show you exactly how to ask existing customers for Google reviews, because reviews are the second most important local ranking signal after the GBP itself.
