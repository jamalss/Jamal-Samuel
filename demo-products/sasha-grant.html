About Sasha

Caribbean Roots.
International Standard.

Sasha Grant was born in Saint Lucia and watched her own family navigate the immigration process — the waiting, the paperwork, the uncertainty of a system that rarely explains itself clearly. That early experience shaped her decision to practise immigration law, and continues to shape how she practises it now.

After completing her law degree and qualifying to practise across multiple jurisdictions, Sasha built a private practice specifically serving Caribbean clients and the diaspora — people who deserve the same calibre of representation available in London, Toronto, or Miami, delivered with an understanding of where they are actually coming from.

Her approach is precise, candid, and unhurried with the details that matter. She does not promise outcomes she cannot guarantee — but she does promise that every client understands exactly where their case stands, at every stage.

Common Questions

Before You
Reach Out

Anything else — ask directly on WhatsApp.

Is my consultation truly confidential?
Yes. Attorney-client privilege considerations apply from the moment you make contact, whether by WhatsApp message or the consultation form. Your information is never shared with third parties, employers, or government bodies without your explicit written consent.
I'm based in the Caribbean. Can Sasha actually help me with US, UK, or Canadian immigration?
Yes. Sasha is admitted to practise in multiple relevant jurisdictions and works specifically with Caribbean-based clients pursuing immigration to the US, UK, and Canada. Many consultations and case preparation stages are conducted remotely via WhatsApp and video call, with in-person meetings arranged when required.
What does the first consultation cost?
Initial enquiries and a brief case orientation are complimentary. A formal paid consultation — typically 45 minutes — is recommended once your situation has been outlined, and fees for this and any onward representation are confirmed in writing before any work begins. There are never hidden costs.
My previous application was refused. Can Sasha still help?
Often, yes. A prior refusal is not the end of the road — it is information. Sasha will review the refusal reasons in detail, identify what went wrong, and advise honestly on whether an appeal, a fresh application, or an alternative pathway gives you the strongest chance of success.
How quickly will my case move?
Timelines depend entirely on the pathway, jurisdiction, and current government processing volumes — which is exactly why the pathway selector tool above gives general ranges rather than promises. Once your case assessment is complete, Sasha will provide a realistic, case-specific timeline rather than a generic estimate.
