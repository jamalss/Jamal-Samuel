The Science

Not all juice is
created equal.

Heat destroys. Most commercial juices are pasteurized at temperatures that eliminate the very enzymes and vitamins that make juice worth drinking. We chose a different path.

01
Harvest at Peak Ripeness

Ingredients are sourced from certified organic farms and harvested at the exact moment nutritional content is highest.

02
Cold Hydraulic Press

We use a two-stage hydraulic press that extracts maximum juice with zero friction heat — preserving delicate enzymes and phytonutrients.

03
HPP Safety, No Compromise

High-pressure processing eliminates harmful bacteria using pure water pressure — not a single degree of heat is applied.

04
Sealed & Delivered Within 24h

Every bottle travels from our press house to your door within one day of pressing. Freshness you can taste.

Our Story

A burnout and a
breakthrough.

"I wasn't looking to start a business. I was looking for something real to drink."
— Miriam Verdant, Founder

In 2019, Miriam Verdant was a healthcare executive running on four hours of sleep and too much coffee. After a wake-up call from her own body, she started driving to Sonoma every Saturday, filling mason jars with produce, and pressing juice in her kitchen for her family.

Friends started asking for bottles. Then their friends. Within six months, there was a waitlist. Within a year, Verdant Press had a press house, three farm partnerships, and a founding team of seven people who believed that access to genuinely good food shouldn't require money or a garden.

Today, Verdant Press is trusted by over 12,000 members, 80+ corporate partners, and some of the most discerning hotel and restaurant brands in the country. But every decision still traces back to a Saturday morning in Sonoma — and a conviction that what we put in our bodies matters enormously.

Questions

Honest answers to
real questions.

How long do cold-pressed juices last?

+

Our juices are HPP-treated, which extends shelf life to 30–45 days from pressing, unopened and refrigerated. Once opened, consume within 48 hours for best quality. We stamp every bottle with a production date and a best-by date so you always know exactly how fresh your juice is.

What does HPP (High Pressure Processing) mean, and is it safe?

+

HPP uses water pressure — not heat — to eliminate harmful bacteria. The sealed bottle is submerged in cold water and subjected to 87,000 PSI of pressure for 3 minutes. This kills pathogens while leaving vitamins, enzymes, and flavour compounds completely intact. It's FDA-cleared and widely used by leading cold-pressed juice brands and premium food producers.

Can I pause or cancel my subscription?

+

Yes — with one click, no phone calls, no penalty. Log into your member dashboard and you can skip a delivery, pause for up to 8 weeks, or cancel permanently. If you cancel within 48 hours of your next delivery, that order will still go out; otherwise changes take effect immediately.

Do you add anything to your juices?

+

Never. What you read on the label is exactly what's in the bottle — nothing more, nothing less. No water, no added sugar, no natural flavours (a legal term that can include dozens of undisclosed compounds), no preservatives, no concentrates. Every bottle is 100% whole fruit and vegetable juice, period.

How do you deliver, and what are your delivery areas?

+

We deliver via our own refrigerated fleet (not third-party couriers) to ensure cold-chain integrity. Same-day delivery is available within a 40-mile radius of our press house for orders placed by 9 AM. Extended next-day delivery is available to a wider area. Enter your zip code at checkout to confirm availability. We ship nationwide for subscription orders only, via overnight refrigerated courier.

Are your juices certified organic?

+

Every ingredient we press carries USDA Certified Organic status. Our press house is a certified organic handling facility, which means the certification covers not just the ingredients but our equipment cleaning protocols, water filtration, and production environment. You can download our current certifications from the Transparency section of our website.

What is your refund policy?

+

If you're not satisfied with any bottle for any reason — quality, flavour, or simply not what you expected — we'll replace it or refund it. No photographs needed, no lengthy forms. Contact our member team within 48 hours of delivery. We stand behind every bottle we press.
