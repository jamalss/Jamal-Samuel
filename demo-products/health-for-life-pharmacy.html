FAQ

Common Questions

Do you fill prescriptions from any doctor?
In most cases, yes. Bring your valid prescription in person or send us a photo on WhatsApp and our pharmacist will confirm the next steps.
Can I transfer my prescription from another pharmacy?
Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your current pharmacy's name and prescription details, and our team will guide you through the transfer.
Do you deliver?
Home delivery is available within our local delivery area. Ask our team on WhatsApp to confirm if we deliver to you.
Can I ask about a medication over WhatsApp?
Yes, for general questions. For anything specific to your personal health or medical history, our pharmacist may ask you to speak in person or by phone.
Do you accept insurance?
Coverage varies by provider. Bring your insurance details in person or ask our team on WhatsApp before your visit.
