Our Story

Pet Lovers, Serving Pet Lovers

Pet's Place started the way most good things on this island do — with a family, a few animals underfoot, and a frustration that quality pet care always seemed to cost more than it should, or come from somewhere far away.

So we built the shop we wished existed. Every product on our shelves is something we'd hand to our own pets without a second thought. Every question gets a real answer, not a sales script.

We're still small enough to know our regulars by name — and their pets by nickname.
