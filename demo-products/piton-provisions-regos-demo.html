      It started with one cassava recipe.

      A family breading mix for Friday fish grew into twelve products cooked in kitchens, market stalls and restaurants around the island.

      Every batch is small and made the same way as the first one. We use cassava, breadfruit and spices grown here, with no artificial preservatives and no imported flavour shortcuts.

      Want to know what's in a mix, or which one works best for your dish? Just ask.
