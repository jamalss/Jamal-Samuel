Between sizes, or shopping for trail or running shoes? Size up half a size — feet swell on long days and you'll want the extra room in the toe box.

How to measure at home

A

Trace your foot

Stand on a sheet of paper, weight evenly distributed, and trace around your foot with a pencil held straight up.

B

Measure heel to toe

Find the longest point of the tracing and measure it in centimeters — that's your foot length.

C

Match to the chart

Match your CM measurement to the size chart. Repeat for both feet and size to the larger one.

D

Check the fit notes

Each product page lists whether that specific style runs true, narrow, or wide — worth a glance before you order.

Our Story

We started because the shoe wall downtown had gotten boring.

Waypoint opened in 2016 as a single rack of shoes we'd personally vouch for, in the back of a friend's coffee shop. Nine years later it's a storefront, but the standard hasn't moved: every brand we carry gets test-walked by someone on staff before it's allowed on the floor.

We're not chasing hype releases or clearing out overstock. We're a small team that answers our own emails, remembers regular customers by name, and would rather talk you out of the wrong shoe than make the sale.

— The Waypoint team
