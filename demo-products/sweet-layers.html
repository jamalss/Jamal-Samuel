👋 Hi, I'm Marva
Meet the Baker
"Every cake begins with a conversation about your colours, your theme, your people, your memory."

I'm Marva, the hands behind every Sweet Layers cake. It started small, baking for my own children's birthdays, then for neighbours, then for people I'd never met who found me through a friend of a friend. Six years later, I'm still in the same kitchen, still mixing every batch myself.

What I've learned is that people don't just want a cake. They want the moment it represents, like a daughter turning one, a couple saying "we do," a graduate who made it through. When you message me on WhatsApp, you're talking to the person who'll actually bake your cake, not a sales team. I want to know your story before I ever pick up a piping bag.

— Marva
