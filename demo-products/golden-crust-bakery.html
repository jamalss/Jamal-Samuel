OUR STORY

Made From Scratch. Never Mass Produced.

Golden Crust started with one oven, one family, and a promise: nothing leaves this kitchen unless we'd serve it to our own. Fifteen years later that promise hasn't changed — every batch is still mixed, shaped, and baked by hand, every single morning.

Customers keep coming back because they can taste the difference: real butter instead of margarine, real fruit instead of flavoring, and bakers who've been perfecting these recipes for over a decade.

2011

One oven, one neighborhood

Golden Crust opens as a two-person kitchen serving fresh bread to the block.

2016

Custom cakes launch

Word spreads after a wedding cake order — custom celebration cakes become a core offering.

2021

Island-wide delivery

Golden Crust begins delivering fresh orders across the island, same-day where possible.

Today

10,000+ customers served

Still family-run, still baking every order fresh, every morning.
