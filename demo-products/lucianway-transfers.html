Airport Pickups

Through customs, straight to your ride

Hewanorra (UVF) is about 90 minutes from the north of the island. After a long flight, the last thing you need is to bargain for a taxi. Here's how it works:

  1. Send your flight numberBook on WhatsApp with your flight, hotel and number of passengers.
  2. We track your flightEarly or delayed, your driver adjusts the pickup time. No extra charge for delays.
  3. Look for your nameYour driver meets you at arrivals with a name sign and helps with your luggage.
