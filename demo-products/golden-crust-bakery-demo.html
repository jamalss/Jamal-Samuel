Our Story

Every Bite Tells a Story

Golden Crust started in a home kitchen in Vieux Fort in 2011, baking bread for neighbors who couldn't find real sourdough on the island. Fifteen years later, we still mix every dough by hand before dawn and never mass-produce a single cake.

No preservatives. No shortcuts. Just butter, flour, patience, and a team that treats every order like it's for family.
