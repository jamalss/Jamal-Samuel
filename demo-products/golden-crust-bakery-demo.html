Our story

Baked by hand, the way it should be

Golden Crust began in a home kitchen, baking bread for neighbours who wanted something better than the supermarket shelf. Today we still mix every dough by hand and bake cakes to order, never in bulk.

No preservatives and no shortcuts. Just butter, flour, patience, and every order treated like it's for family.

  • Cakes baked to your date, not pulled from a freezer
  • Every order confirmed personally on WhatsApp

Ask on WhatsApp

How far in advance should I order a custom cake?
Birthday cakes need at least 3 days' notice, wedding cakes 2 weeks, and large event orders 1 week. Rush orders are sometimes possible, so just ask on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver island-wide?
Yes. Same-day delivery is available nearby, and other areas usually take one extra day. Delivery fees are quoted by distance when you order.

Can you accommodate dietary needs?
Some items can be made egg-free or with reduced gluten. Tell us about any allergies when you order and we'll tell you honestly what we can and can't do.

How do I pay?
Cash on pickup or delivery, or bank transfer for larger custom orders. We confirm the details with you on WhatsApp before anything is baked.
