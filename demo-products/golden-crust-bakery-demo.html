FAQ

Common questions

Something else? Ask us on WhatsApp.

Ask on WhatsApp

How far ahead should I order?
Order at least one day ahead so your loaves are baked fresh for you. For gift boxes or large orders, a few days' notice is best.

Are you open on Saturdays?
No. We're closed on Saturdays, so pickups and deliveries run Sunday to Friday. The order form won't let you pick a Saturday.

How long does banana bread stay fresh?
Kept wrapped at room temperature it's best within a few days. It keeps longer in the fridge and freezes well, so slice and freeze what you won't finish. Every box comes with a care card.

Can I send a loaf as a gift?
Yes. Choose any flavour, add a gift note in the order form, and it goes out wrapped with a ribbon and care card.

Do you deliver?
Pickup is free. Delivery is available, and the fee depends on where you are. We confirm it on WhatsApp before your order is baked.

How do I pay?
Cash on pickup or delivery, or bank transfer. We confirm payment details with you on WhatsApp.
