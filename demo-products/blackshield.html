Know Your Protection

Understanding Auto Protection

Informed clients make better decisions. Here's what matters.

What is Paint Protection Film (PPF)?
PPF is a clear thermoplastic urethane film applied to the exterior of a vehicle to protect paint from damage. High-quality films feature a self-healing top coat that removes minor scratches when exposed to heat. Think of it as an invisible armour over your paint — absorbing impacts so your paint doesn't have to.
Why Choose Ceramic Coating?
Unlike traditional wax that sits on top of paint and degrades in weeks, ceramic coating chemically bonds to the clear coat — creating a permanent, extremely hard layer. The result: water sheets off, dirt struggles to bond, and your paint maintains depth and gloss for years, not months.
Heat Rejection vs. UV Protection
These are not the same thing. UV protection blocks ultraviolet rays that fade upholstery and damage skin. Heat rejection measures total solar energy blocked — it includes infrared rays that make cabins feel like ovens. Premium ceramic films deliver both: up to 99% UV and 60–70% heat rejection simultaneously.
Maintenance Tips
New tint: wait 3–5 days before rolling windows down. New PPF or ceramic: avoid pressure washing the edges for 7 days. For ongoing care — hand wash preferred, pH-neutral soap, no automatic car washes with rotating brushes. We provide a full aftercare card with every installation.

Common Myths — Corrected

"Ceramic coating is scratch-proof." — It resists light scratches. It is not impervious to keys or abrasives. PPF handles that job better.

"Window tint is just for looks." — Quality films do measurable work: reducing UV, heat, glare, and interior fade.

PPF and ceramic coating complement each other perfectly — PPF protects from physical damage; ceramic handles chemical resistance and gloss.

"All films are basically the same." — There is a significant performance difference between budget and premium-grade materials. Film quality determines longevity.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked

How long does window tint last? +
Quality ceramic and carbon films last 10+ years or the lifetime of the vehicle when maintained correctly. Budget dye-based films typically degrade and purple within 3–5 years. We only install premium films specifically because of longevity.
Is window tint legal? +
Tint laws vary by region and regulate VLT percentages per window position. We are familiar with regulations in our service area and will always recommend legal configurations. You are responsible for verifying local laws. We can advise, but we cannot guarantee legality across all jurisdictions.
How long does PPF last? +
Premium PPF films carry manufacturer warranties of 10–12 years. With proper care — regular hand washing, avoiding high-pressure jets on film edges, and avoiding harsh chemicals — films routinely outlast their warranty period significantly.
Can PPF be removed? +
Yes. PPF can be professionally removed without damaging the underlying paint — provided the paint surface was in good condition before installation and the film is not excessively aged. We offer professional PPF removal and can advise on timing and feasibility for your specific vehicle.
How do I care for ceramic coating? +
Hand wash with pH-neutral soap and a clean microfibre mitt. Avoid automated car washes with rotating brushes — they create swirl marks even on coated surfaces. Use ceramic-safe maintenance sprays between washes. We provide a full aftercare guide with every ceramic installation.
Is paint correction necessary before ceramic coating? +
It depends on paint condition. Ceramic coating locks in your paint's current state — it doesn't hide defects. If your paint has swirl marks, scratches, or oxidation, correction is strongly recommended before coating. We'll assess and advise honestly after the inspection.
How long after tint installation can I roll down my windows? +
Wait at least 3–5 days before lowering tinted windows, and longer in cooler weather. The adhesive requires time to cure fully. Rolling windows down too early can cause the film to peel from the edge. We'll confirm the correct wait time based on the day's conditions.
Will ceramic coating interfere with my Tesla's sensors? +
Professional ceramic coatings applied correctly do not interfere with cameras, ultrasonic sensors, or radar systems. We have extensive experience with Tesla vehicles and take particular care around sensor locations. Tint film is not applied to sensor zones either.
How do I book an appointment? +
The fastest way is WhatsApp — message us directly for a same-day or next-day response. Alternatively, use the booking form on this page. We'll confirm your slot, send the address and prep instructions, and you're set.
Do you offer same-day tinting? +
Same-day tinting is available subject to current scheduling. Contact us early in the day to check availability. Larger jobs (PPF, ceramic, full details) are typically scheduled 2–5 days in advance to ensure bay time is available.
What's the difference between PPF and ceramic coating? +
PPF is a physical barrier — a thick urethane film that absorbs rock chips, scratches, and abrasion. Ceramic coating is a chemical bond that enhances gloss, repels water, and resists environmental contamination. They address different threats and work exceptionally well together.
Can you install PPF on a wrapped vehicle? +
Yes — PPF can be installed over a vinyl wrap. This is increasingly popular for colour-changed vehicles where the owner wants to protect the wrap investment. PPF installation over vinyl requires additional care and slightly different technique; our installers are experienced with this combination.
How long does PPF installation take? +
Front-end packages (bumper, leading edge, mirrors): 1 day. Full front package (hood, fenders, bumper): 1–2 days. Full body PPF: 2–4 days depending on vehicle size and complexity. We'll confirm your timeline when booking.
Is there a warranty if something goes wrong? +
Yes — all work carries both a manufacturer material warranty and our own workmanship guarantee. If an installation defect is identified (not caused by external damage or incorrect aftercare), we will rectify it at no charge. See our Warranty section for full details.
Do you work on leased vehicles? +
Yes. PPF is especially popular on leased vehicles because it protects the paint for the duration of the lease and can be removed cleanly before return — helping you avoid costly damage fees. Tinting a leased vehicle is also common and generally permitted; check your lease agreement first.
How should I maintain PPF? +
Hand wash with pH-neutral soap, avoid pressure-washing edges directly, and don't use wax-based products on PPF (they can leave residue). Self-healing activation: pour warm water over light scratches. We provide a full PPF aftercare guide at handover.
Can I wash my car immediately after PPF or ceramic installation? +
For PPF: avoid washing for 7 days. For ceramic: avoid washing for 7–14 days (depends on the product used). During curing, avoid rain exposure as much as possible. We'll confirm the exact aftercare protocol based on what was applied to your vehicle.
Do you offer mobile service? +
Full PPF and ceramic installations require our controlled indoor bay. Mobile service is available for minor services such as dash cam installation or specific add-ons. For any film or coating work, bay installation is required to ensure quality — we don't compromise on that.
Do you offer fleet pricing? +
Yes — we offer custom fleet pricing for businesses requiring multiple vehicles. Fleet rates apply from 3 vehicles and scale with volume. Contact us directly on WhatsApp to discuss your fleet needs and we'll prepare a tailored proposal.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept bank transfer, card, and cash. A deposit is required to secure your booking slot. The balance is due on completion, after your inspection of the finished work. We never ask for full payment before the job begins.
