Foot Treatments

Your feet carry you all day. Treat them.

Sandals, sand and heat dry out heels faster than most people expect. Our pedicures and heel treatments leave feet soft, neat and comfortable, and they pair easily with a facial in the same visit.

Diabetic, pregnant, or have a foot infection or ingrown nail? Let us know when you book. Some foot concerns need a doctor or podiatrist first, and we'll tell you honestly if that's the case.
