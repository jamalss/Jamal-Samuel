OUR STORY

Made From Scratch. Never Mass Produced.

Golden Crust started with one oven, one family, and a promise: nothing leaves this kitchen unless we'd serve it to our own. Fifteen years later that promise hasn't changed.

Customers keep coming back because they can taste the difference: real butter instead of margarine, real fruit instead of flavoring, and bakers who've spent over a decade perfecting these recipes.
