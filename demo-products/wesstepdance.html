Our Foundation

Building Dancers.
Honouring Legacy.

"Technique · Culture · Performance"

WesStep was founded on a single belief: that Caribbean dance is not just movement — it is memory, identity, and expression. Our programs are built to develop technically skilled, culturally grounded performers who carry that legacy forward.

Whether you are stepping on stage for the first time or refining years of practice, WesStep meets you where you are and builds you into something more.

Technique
Rigorous, progressive training in Caribbean, modern, and contemporary forms.
Culture
Deep roots in Afro-diasporic traditions. Every movement carries meaning.
Performance
Real stage experience culminating in a live community showcase.
Community
Bonds built through shared sweat, discipline, and creative growth.

WesStep believes that financial circumstance should not determine who has access to transformative dance training. Scholarships are available for the Summer 2026 Intensive to ensure every qualified dancer can participate.

Scholarship Considerations
  • Demonstrated commitment to dance and the arts
  • Financial need or limited access to training opportunities
  • Written statement of intent and artistic goals
  • Recommendation or reference (where available)
  • Willingness to participate fully in all program activities
