FAQ & Pickup Details

Good to know before you order.

Orders are weekly only and close every Saturday at 10 AM. Payment is collected in full at the time of order to confirm your meals.
Pickup is Sunday in Charlotte, NC. Weekday pickup after 5:30 PM may be available on request — reach out if that works better for you.
Accommodations are available. Meals are prepared in a home kitchen where common allergens may be present — if you have a severe allergy, please reach out before ordering.
Weekly Meal Prep follows the chef's set weekly menu. Custom Meal Prep is built around your specific preferences, dislikes, or health goals.
Yes — Weekly Meal Prep runs as an ongoing weekly order. You can cancel anytime before the weekly order cutoff. Custom Meal Prep, Chef Services, and Events are one-time orders.
All sales are final due to the nature of food preparation. Weekly Meal Prep subscriptions can be canceled before the weekly cutoff to prevent future charges; completed or upcoming confirmed orders are non-refundable.
