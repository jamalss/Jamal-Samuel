Our Story

Brewed with Purpose,
Served with Soul

Velour Café was born in 2019 from a simple conviction: Saint Lucia deserved a coffee experience that matched the beauty of the island itself. Unhurried. Intentional. Deeply good.

We source single-origin beans from small ethical farms, roast in small batches, and train our team to treat every order as a craft. Nothing here is rushed. Everything is made with intention.

Whether you're starting your morning with us or winding down your afternoon, you'll find the same warmth in every cup — because we believe the best things in life deserve to be savoured.
