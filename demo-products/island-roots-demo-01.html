Helping Your Plants Thrive.

Simple guidance can make choosing and caring for plants easier. It does not replace species-specific care advice.

Watering Without Overwatering

Check the top few centimetres of soil before watering. If it still feels damp, wait. Use pots with drainage holes and empty any water left in saucers. Many common plants suffer more from too much water than too little, but needs differ by species.

Understanding Light Requirements

Bright light means near a sunny window or outdoors. Indirect light is bright but away from harsh direct sun. Shade-tolerant plants cope with lower light, though few grow well in true darkness. Watch how a plant responds and move it gradually.

Choosing Plants for Your Space

Start with the light in the spot, then think about how much time you want to spend on care. Measure the space, allow for the plant’s mature size, and consider pets or children, since some plants can be harmful if eaten.
