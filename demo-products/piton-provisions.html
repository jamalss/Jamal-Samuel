Our Story

Real Flavour. No Shortcuts.

"We built Piton Provisions because Caribbean cooking deserves Caribbean ingredients and not generic mixes from a warehouse shelf."

What started as a family recipe for cassava breading has grown into a full range of breading mixes, dry rubs and sauces that carry the authentic taste of Saint Lucia. Our products are used in home kitchens, market stalls and restaurants across the island.

Every batch is small, intentional and made with the same care as the first one. No artificial preservatives. No imported flavour substitutes. Just Saint Lucia, in a bag.
