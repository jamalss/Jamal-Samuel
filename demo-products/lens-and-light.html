Behind the Camera

Hi, I'm Alex Bellrose.

I started shooting weddings on borrowed gear on the north coast of Saint Lucia, and I've spent the years since chasing the same thing: the moment someone stops posing and just is. Not the stiff, staged version of a day — the real one.

I work across the island and the wider Caribbean, from cliffside ceremonies in Soufrière to quiet portrait sessions in Rodney Bay. My approach is simple — I stay close, I stay quiet, and I let the day happen. The camera just makes sure you don't have to remember it alone.
