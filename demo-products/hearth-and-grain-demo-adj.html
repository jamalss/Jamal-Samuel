    Three ingredients and a lot of time

    Flour
    Unbleached bread flour, with whole wheat and rye for depth and color.
    Water
    Filtered, and plenty of it. High hydration is what opens up the crumb.
    Salt
    Sea salt for flavor and structure. That's the whole list for the country loaf.

    No commercial yeast, no preservatives, no dough conditioners. The rise comes from a starter fed every morning, and each dough ferments for about 36 hours before it meets the oven. That slow ferment is where the flavor comes from.

    Good to know

    How long does sourdough keep?

    Three to four days on the counter, cut side down or in a bread bag. To bring back a day-old loaf, warm it in a 350°F oven for 5 to 8 minutes.

    Can I freeze it?

    Yes. Slice it first, freeze it in a sealed bag for up to three months, and toast slices straight from the freezer.

    Can you slice my loaf?

    Yes. Add "sliced" in the note on your order.

    How do I pay?

    Pay at pickup with cash or card, or use the payment link in your confirmation text. Nothing is charged when you send your order.

    What if I can't make my pickup?

    Text before the pickup window ends and we'll work out another time. Orders that aren't picked up are held until the end of that day.

    Is it safe for allergies?

    Everything is made in a home kitchen that also handles wheat, dairy, eggs, tree nuts and seeds. Every loaf's label lists its ingredients. If you have a serious allergy, text before ordering.
