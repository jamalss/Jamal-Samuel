The Artist

I paint what
words cannot hold

I've always believed that the most honest things — grief, wonder, desire, relief — live beyond language. Abstract painting is where I put them.

My work draws from lived experience: the tension between stillness and movement, the way colour behaves when you stop trying to control it. Each canvas begins as an impulse and becomes its own kind of truth.

I take a limited number of commissions each year — pieces made with the same intention and care as every original in this gallery.
