Questions we get every week

How do I book?

Fill in the booking builder above and tap the WhatsApp button. Your message opens ready to send. We reply with available dates and the deposit details.

Do I need to bring my own braiding hair?

No. Hair is included in the starting price for most styles. If you prefer a certain brand or colour, choose "I'll bring my own" in the builder or tell us in your message.

How should my hair be when I arrive?

Washed, completely dry and detangled, with no heavy oils or gels. Wash a day or two before, not the morning of. If you need a wash on the day, add it when you book.

How long will I be in the chair?

Cornrows take 1 to 3 hours. Most box braids, knotless, twists and locs take 4 to 8 hours depending on size and length. We give you a time estimate when we confirm.

Will my scalp hurt?

Braids should never be painfully tight. Tell us straight away if anything pulls. Knotless styles are the gentlest option if you have a sensitive scalp.

Do you do home or travel appointments?

Yes, on request. A travel fee is added depending on the area. Mention it in your booking message.
