Our Story

BORN IN DALLAS,
BUILT FOR GAMEDAY

Est. 2011 · Dallas, TX

Gridiron Kitchen started with a smoker, a parking lot, and a line around the block. Thirteen years later, we're still cooking the same way — slow, hot, and without shortcuts.

When Dallas was named a World Cup host city, we knew what that meant: 180,000 fans from 48 nations, all of them hungry. We built a menu that speaks every language — through smoke, spice, and quality ingredients.

Whether you're here for the football, the food, or both — you're welcome at this table.
