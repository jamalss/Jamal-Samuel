The Story

Charlotte. Faith.
Basketball.

"I never played just to play. I played to prove something — to every girl from Charlotte who was told the dream was too big."

Avery Brooks grew up in the east side of Charlotte, North Carolina, where basketball wasn't a hobby — it was a language. The third of four siblings, she learned discipline at the kitchen table and resilience on cracked outdoor courts, long before anyone was watching.

By her sophomore year of high school she was being recruited nationally. By her junior year she was a McDonald's All-American nominee. But the decision wasn't just about basketball. Avery chose Westbrook University because of its culture of faith, community, and the expectation that athletes leave the program better people — not just better players.

Three years in, she leads the team in points and assists, mentors freshmen formally and informally, and is building a personal brand centered on the belief that sports are a platform — not a destination.

Career

The Journey So Far

Charlotte, NC · Early Years
Where It Began
Discovered basketball at age seven through an after-school program. By 13 she was playing AAU ball across the country. Two older brothers taught her to play without fear.
High School · Myers Park Academy
State Champion · 2× All-State Guard
Led Myers Park to back-to-back state championships as a junior and senior. Named team captain her sophomore year — the first in school history at that age. 4-star recruit. 28 Division I offers.
Westbrook University · Year One
Conference Freshman of the Year
Started all 34 games. Averaged 14.2 points and 5.1 assists. Named Conference Freshman of the Year. Helped Westbrook reach the Sweet 16 for the first time in 11 years.
Westbrook University · Year Two
All-Conference First Team
Elevated to team captain. Led conference in assists. Career-high 34-point performance in March. Westbrook reached the Elite Eight. Named to Academic All-Conference team.
Westbrook University · Year Three
3× All-Conference · Conference POY Finalist
18.4 PPG, 6.2 APG, 2.8 SPG. Conference Player of the Year finalist. Led Westbrook to their first conference championship since 2009. WNBA Draft prospect. Currently active season.
2025 → Beyond
Professional Career · Legacy Building
WNBA Draft prospect. Goal: professional basketball career across multiple seasons and leagues — while building a personal brand, foundation, and community program that lasts decades beyond the final buzzer.

🏀 Annual Charlotte Youth Camp
Every summer, Avery returns to Charlotte to host a free skills camp for girls ages 8–16. Over 200 athletes attended in 2024. No cost. No roster limit. Every girl plays.
📚 Study Hall Initiative
A partnership with three Charlotte middle schools providing tutoring, mentorship, and college-prep support for student-athletes who lack access to resources but have the drive to succeed.
🎤 Leadership Workshop Series
School visits, keynotes, and panel discussions on leadership, mental health, faith, and navigating high-pressure environments — delivered to students, athletes, and young professionals.
🤝 NIL with Purpose
Every brand partnership Avery considers must have a community component — whether that's a percentage donated, a clinic co-sponsored, or a scholarship funded. No exceptions.
🙏 Faith & Wellness Programming
Regular mental health check-ins, faith-based reflection sessions, and wellness conversations hosted for teammates and community youth groups throughout the academic year.
🏆 Scholarship Fund
